Place your Bloody Hell @font-face kit files in this directory:
- BloodyHell.woff2 (preferred)
- BloodyHell.woff
- BloodyHell.ttf

The app automatically loads them via @font-face and pre-caches them in the service worker for offline use.
When the font files are added, the app name, screen titles, section titles, and big headings will automatically render with Bloody Hell!
Fallback font stack: 'Bloody Hell', system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif.
